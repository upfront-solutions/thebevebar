FAQs

How much does drink cart catering cost?

Package One is $875 for 75 drinks (12oz) and Package Two is $1,000 for 100 drinks (12oz), both with free customized cup stickers. For a custom order, it’s a $500 cart rental plus $5 per 12oz cup or $7 per 16oz cup, with a 50 cup minimum. Use our booking form to see your exact estimate.

What’s included with the cart?

Every booking includes 2 hours of service plus setup, breakdown, and delivery. Extra hours are $100 each.

Do you offer boba and other toppings?

Yes! Add drink toppings like boba, jelly, or fruit bits. See our full menu for every tea, matcha, and coffee option.

Can we name the drinks on our menu?

Yes! Every menu can be customized with your own drink names. We hang each name right on the tap.

Can you add our logo to the cart or cups?

Yes. Customized cup stickers are free with Package One and Two. Vinyl signage starts at $55 and a vinyl wrap starts at $100.

Do I need a barista?

You can choose a self-serve cart or full service with a barista. Baristas are $100 per hour, and events with 100+ guests need at least one. Package Two includes a free 2-hour barista.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Orange County, Los Angeles, San Diego, and surrounding areas. The first 10 miles from Anaheim are free, then $3 per mile. Events 60+ miles away get a custom quote.

Do you do weddings and corporate events?

Yes! We serve weddings, corporate events, tradeshows, birthday parties, graduations, and more.

Can we get drinks without the cart?

Yes. Choose cup catering (drinks delivered only) or order by the gallon. Our ParTea Jugs are $50 per full gallon.

How do I book?

Fill out the booking form to see your estimate, and our team will contact you to confirm. A 50% deposit secures your date, and cancellations made 7 days before the event get a full refund.
